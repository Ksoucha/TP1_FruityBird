# FruityBird
A mini-game that follows the style of the Suika game. 

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3114b07c-4126-464e-8836-b071219596d3)

## Features
* Fruits randomly spawn underneath the player, and they must be dropped in their corresponding box
* There are spikes that are constantly moving from right to left and they can prevent the fruits from falling into their corresponding box
* There is a score to keep track of the fruits that successfully fell into their box
* The player has a moving restriction, meaning they can only move freely inside a certain area
